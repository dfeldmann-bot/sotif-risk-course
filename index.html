SOTIF Risk Course

Quantitative risk calculation and validation under ISO 21448, from acceptance criteria to the residual-risk argument you can defend in front of an assessor.

Mission. Build and defend the quantitative residual-risk argument for an L4 system: acceptance criteria → validation targets → evidence. This includes total and per-scenario risk from the scenario database, and where runtime risk models (SINADRA, monitor ensembles) fit in.
  1. 01From acceptance criterion to validation targetOpenAnnex C decomposition, the Poisson zero-failure test, k-failure plans, and five hidden assumptions.
  2. 02Testing designs that survive realityNextTest-fix-retest, Wald’s SPRT, Bayesian priors and what an assessor will accept.
  3. 03Allocating the risk budgetPlannedBonferroni over trigger → behaviour → event paths; QRN-style incident budgets.
  4. 04Scenario-weighted riskPlannedExposure × P(harm | scenario); per-scenario contribution; what your scenario database must store.
  5. 05Dependent parameters with copulasPlannedSklar’s theorem, fitting, and Monte Carlo over correlated scenario parameters.
  6. 06Rare eventsPlannedImportance sampling, cross-entropy, extreme value analysis.
  7. 07Runtime risk: SINADRA and monitor ensemblesPlannedSEM → CBCT → Bayesian network; what runtime evidence can and cannot add to the argument.
  8. 08Assessor drillsPlannedAttack and defend quantitative claims under time pressure.
