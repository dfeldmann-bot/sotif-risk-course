From Acceptance Criteria to Test Hours: Budget Allocation and the Role of Importance Sampling in SOTIF Validation

Course note · SOTIF risk course · 30 October 2026
Abstract. The residual risk of an automated driving function is the sum, over hazardous behaviours, of their rate times the harm each occurrence causes. Splitting this sum lets the rate be shown by driving and the harm per occurrence by simulation. The split, not importance sampling, is what reduces the required driving by a factor of order \(1/W\). Importance sampling only makes the simulated harm term cheap to estimate. We state the six equations that lead from the acceptance criteria to test hours and simulation runs.

1Risk per acceptance criterion

For each severity class \(s\) with acceptance criterion \(A_s\) (harmed persons per hour), the residual risk must satisfy

\[ R_s \;=\; \sum_{b} r_b \, W_{b,s} \;\le\; A_s . \]
(1)

Here \(r_b\) is the rate of hazardous behaviour \(b\) per hour and \(W_{b,s}\) the expected number of persons harmed at severity \(\ge s\) per occurrence of \(b\).

2Harm per hazardous behaviour

Following ISO 21448 [1, C.2.1, Formula (C.1)], \(W\) decomposes over the hazardous events \(e\) that \(b\) can cause:

\[ W_{b,s} \;=\; \sum_{e} P_{E\mid b}\; P_{\bar C\mid E}\; \pi_{e,s}, \]
(2)

with exposure \(P_{E\mid b}\), probability of not controlling \(P_{\bar C\mid E}\), and severity fraction \(\pi_{e,s}\) obtained from \(\Delta v\) and an injury-risk function [2]. Each factor is below one, so typically \(W \sim 10^{-2}\text{–}10^{-4}\).

3Budget allocation

The validation targets \(r_b^{\max}\) are chosen to minimise total test effort subject to (1):

\[ \min_{r_1,\dots,r_n}\; \sum_{b=1}^{n} \frac{c_b\,\lambda_{k,b}}{r_b} \]
(3a)
\[ \text{subject to}\quad \sum_{b=1}^{n} r_b\,W_{b,s} \;\le\; A_s \quad \text{for all } s , \]
(3b)

where \(c_b\) is the cost of one test hour and \(\lambda_{k,b}\) the statistical factor of Eq. (4). Problem (3) is convex; a solver returns \(r_b^{\max}\) and the binding class for each \(b\). For a single criterion \(A\), the Lagrange condition gives the closed form

\[ B_b \;=\; A\,\frac{\sqrt{c_b\,\lambda_{k,b}\,W_b}}{\sum_{i=1}^{n}\sqrt{c_i\,\lambda_{k,i}\,W_i}}, \qquad r_b^{\max} \;=\; \frac{B_b}{W_b}. \]
(3c)

The budget \(B_b\) is what behaviour \(b\) may contribute; \(r_b^{\max}\) is its validation target. The equal split \(B_b = A/n\) is feasible but not optimal [3, 4].

4Driving or replay hours

Showing \(r_b \le r_b^{\max}\) at confidence \(C\) with \(k\) observed events requires

\[ T_b \;=\; \frac{\lambda_{k,b}}{r_b^{\max}}, \qquad \lambda_{k} \;=\; \tfrac12\,\chi^2_{2(k+1)}(C), \qquad \lambda_0 \approx 3 \text{ at } C = 95\% . \]
(4)

Targeted driving in trigger contexts \(j\) (low sun, spray, gantries) is admissible if re-weighted by the exposure shares \(\nu_j\):

\[ r_b \;=\; \sum_j \nu_j\, r_{b,j} . \]
(5)

5Simulation runs and importance sampling

The conditional probabilities in (2) are estimated by simulating logical scenarios with parameters \(\theta\) drawn from a proposal \(q\) that over-samples critical conditions, re-weighted to the real-world density \(f\) [1, C.5]:

\[ \hat P \;=\; \frac{1}{N}\sum_{i=1}^{N} \frac{f(\theta_i)}{q(\theta_i)}\,h_i, \qquad \mathrm{RSE} \;=\; \frac{\operatorname{sd}\!\big(h\,f/q\big)}{\sqrt{N}\,\hat P} . \]
(6)

\(h_i\) is the outcome of run \(i\) (collision indicator or injury probability). Non-critical runs enter with their weights and are part of the estimate. \(N\) is increased until the RSE is acceptable; the upper bound \(\bar W_{b,s}\) is used in the verdict.

6Verdict

\[ \sum_{b} \bar r_b\, \bar W_{b,s} \;\le\; A_s \quad \text{for all } s, \qquad \sum_i (1 - C_i) \;\le\; 1 - C . \]
(7)

Bars denote upper confidence bounds; the second condition is the Bonferroni split of the error probability over all estimated terms [3]. The verdict is on the sum: one behaviour may exceed \(r_b^{\max}\) if others leave slack.

7Where the reduction comes from

Importance sampling does not reduce driving hours. The decomposition (1)–(2) does, by the factor \(1/W\); importance sampling reduces the number of simulation runs needed to estimate \(W\).
