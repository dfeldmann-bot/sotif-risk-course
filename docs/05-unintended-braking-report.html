References and further reading

Standards

  • ISO 21448:2022, Road vehicles — Safety of the intended functionality. Clauses 6, 9–13, Annex C.
  • ISO/TS 5083:2025, Road vehicles — Safety for automated driving systems — Design, verification and validation.
  • ISO 34502:2022, Scenario based safety evaluation framework; ISO 34504, Scenario categorization.
  • ISO 15622, Adaptive cruise control systems — Performance requirements and test procedures.
  • ANSI/UL 4600, Standard for Safety for the Evaluation of Autonomous Products, Ch. 16 (SPIs).

Papers and reports

  • Putze, Westhofen, Koopmann, Böde, Neurohr (2023). On Quantification for SOTIF Validation of Automated Driving Systems. arXiv:2304.10170.
  • Betschinske et al. (2025). Towards more efficient quantitative safety validation of residual risk for assisted and automated driving. arXiv:2506.10363.
  • Kalra, Paddock (2016). Driving to Safety. RAND RR-1478. rand.org.
  • Zhao et al. (2017). Accelerated evaluation of automated vehicles safety in lane-change scenarios based on importance sampling. arXiv:1605.04965.
  • de Gelder (2022). Safety assessment of automated vehicles using real-world driving scenarios. PhD thesis, TU Delft.
  • Ratiu, Rohlinger, Stolte, Wagner (2024). Towards an Argument Pattern for the Use of Safety Performance Indicators. arXiv:2410.00578.
  • Koopman. SPI vs KPI, CMU lecture notes. PDF.
  • Schaller et al. (2026). Addressing SOTIF with a runtime monitor ensemble for AI-based perception systems. Discover AI 6:1172. doi.
  • Reich (2026). SINADRA: Situation-Aware Dynamic Risk Assessment for Safe and Permissive Autonomous Systems. Dr.-Ing. thesis, RPTU Kaiserslautern-Landau.
  • Au, Beck (2001). Estimation of small failure probabilities in high dimensions by subset simulation. Probabilistic Engineering Mechanics 16(4).

Statistics books for deeper understanding

  • Wasserman (2004). All of Statistics. Springer. Compact, rigorous tour of inference, written for scientists and engineers. Start here.
  • Casella, Berger (2002). Statistical Inference, 2nd ed. Duxbury. The standard text on estimators, confidence intervals and hypothesis tests (lessons 1–2).
  • Meeker, Hahn, Escobar (2017). Statistical Intervals: A Guide for Practitioners and Researchers, 2nd ed. Wiley. Exact Poisson and binomial bounds, zero-failure demonstration tests.
  • Rausand, Barros, Høyland (2020). System Reliability Theory: Models, Statistical Methods, and Applications, 3rd ed. Wiley. Failure rates, demonstration testing, Bayesian reliability.
  • Gelman et al. (2013). Bayesian Data Analysis, 3rd ed. CRC. Priors, posteriors, hierarchical models. Free PDF on the authors' page.
  • Owen (2013). Monte Carlo theory, methods and examples. Ch. 9–10: importance sampling, diagnostics, effective sample size. Chapters online at artowen.su.domains/mc.
  • Robert, Casella (2004). Monte Carlo Statistical Methods, 2nd ed. Springer. Deeper theory of simulation-based estimation.
  • Rubinstein, Kroese (2016). Simulation and the Monte Carlo Method, 3rd ed. Wiley. Rare events and the cross-entropy method for choosing proposals.
  • Nelsen (2006). An Introduction to Copulas, 2nd ed. Springer. Sklar's theorem, Kendall's τ, tail dependence.
  • Joe (2014). Dependence Modeling with Copulas. CRC. Vine copulas, fitting, high-dimensional dependence.
  • Coles (2001). An Introduction to Statistical Modeling of Extreme Values. Springer. GPD tails and threshold choice for near-miss extrapolation.
