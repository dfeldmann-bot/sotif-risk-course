Gaussian copula

Coupling scenario parameters while keeping their own distributions: sampling, density, fitting, and the tail-dependence caveat.

1. Scenario-based residual risk2. Field data and SPIs3. Gaussian copula4. How the 1,000 scenarios were generated5. Unintended braking report6. Scaling up: ACC and L4 robotaxi7. Scenario database, ODD coverage and completeness

How the scenario parameters are coupled in the example, and how to do it with real data. The live version, with editable \(R\), Cholesky factor in formulas and a 200-draw demo, is the sheet Gaussian Copula in the workbook. Code: sample_theta(), log_density() in tools/ub_sim.py.

1 · What it is

A copula is a joint distribution on \([0,1]^d\) with uniform marginals. Sklar's theorem: every joint CDF splits into marginals and a copula:

\[ F(x_1,\dots,x_d) = C\big(F_1(x_1),\dots,F_d(x_d)\big), \qquad C_R(u) = \Phi_R\big(\Phi^{-1}(u_1),\dots,\Phi^{-1}(u_d)\big) \]

Each scenario parameter keeps its natural marginal (uniform speed in a band, lognormal reaction time, …), and \(R\) alone sets how the parameters move together.

2 · Sampling

\[ \varepsilon\sim\mathcal N(0,I),\quad Z = L\varepsilon\ \ (R = LL^\top),\quad U_i = \Phi(Z_i),\quad X_i = F_i^{-1}(U_i) \]

Correlation matrix used in the example (illustrative):

\(v_e\)\(h\)\(T_r\)\(a_f\)
\(v_e\)+1.00+0.25+0.00+0.00
\(h\)+0.25+1.00-0.30+0.00
\(T_r\)+0.00-0.30+1.00-0.40
\(a_f\)+0.00+0.00-0.40+1.00

The signs encode an assumed story: faster traffic keeps slightly larger time gaps; close followers are more alert; late reactors also brake less hard.

3 · Density (needed for importance weights)

\[ c_R(u) = |R|^{-1/2}\exp\!\Big(-\tfrac12\, z^\top (R^{-1}-I)\, z\Big),\quad z=\Phi^{-1}(u), \qquad f(x) = c_R\big(F(x)\big)\prod_i f_i(x_i) \]

The weights \(w = f(\theta)/q(\theta)\) evaluate this for both models. Under the proposal the same \(R\) is applied to q's marginals, so the copula terms do not cancel in the ratio.

4 · Fitting \(R\) from field data

  • Pseudo-observations \(u = \text{rank}/(n+1)\), normal scores \(z=\Phi^{-1}(u)\), and \(R = \operatorname{corr}(z)\).
  • Pairwise: Kendall's \(\tau\) → \(\rho = \sin(\pi\tau/2)\). Rank-based fits do not depend on the marginals.
  • Validity: \(R\) symmetric positive definite (\(\det R>0\), the Cholesky factor exists). Otherwise project to the nearest correlation matrix.

5 · Main limitation: no tail dependence

\[ \lambda_U = \lim_{q\to1} P\big(U_2>q \mid U_1>q\big) = 0 \ \text{for the Gaussian copula }(\rho \lt 1), \qquad \lambda_U = 2\,t_{\nu+1}\!\Big(-\sqrt{\tfrac{(\nu+1)(1-\rho)}{1+\rho}}\Big) > 0 \ \text{for the t-copula} \]
