Validation targets

Acceptance criterion → tolerable behaviour rate → test exposure. Taught in Lesson 1.

Decomposition (ISO 21448:2022, C.2.1, Formulas (C.1)–(C.2), p. 127)

RHB = AH / [ P(E | HB) · P(C̄ | E) · P(S | C̄) ]

Correct conditioning: P(S | C̄, E, HB). Every factor is system-dependent (route, ODD, fallback) and needs its own justification.

Scaling rules

  • τ ∝ 1/R: one decade in the target rate costs one decade of exposure.
  • τ ∝ −ln(1 − C): 95% → 99% costs ×1.54.
  • Allowing k = 1 costs ×1.58 at 95%. Fix k and τ before testing.

Assumption checklist (ask these of any passed test)

  1. Homogeneous Poisson: constant rate and independent events?
  2. Representative exposure: test mix = deployment mix, per ODD stratum?
  3. Frozen software: all hours on the release candidate, or a carry-over argument?
  4. Aggregation: all HB paths to harm H counted, with the budget allocated?
  5. Confidence semantics: frequentist error bound, or posterior with a stated prior?
  6. Notation: is “α” confidence (ISO 21448 Formula (C.7), p. 137) or an error level (ISO 21448 C.6.3.2, p. 156; Betschinske)?
